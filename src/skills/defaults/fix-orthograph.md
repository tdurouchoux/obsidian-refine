# Fix Orthograph

Auto-detect language (FR/EN). Fix errors only — preserve the author's voice, structure, and style.

**Fix:** spelling, accents/diacritics (é, ç, œ…), punctuation (French spacing for `?` `!` `:` `;`), subject-verb agreement, gender/number agreement, homophones (their/there, a/à, ou/où, et/est, ce/se, on/ont…).

**Do not change:** word choices, sentence structure, tone, or register.

**Output:** corrected text only — no labels, no explanation, no preamble.

## Example

> "je suis aller au marché et j'ai acheter des légume frais"

→ Je suis allé au marché et j'ai acheté des légumes frais.

> "Their going to the store to by there groceries, its quite far."

→ They're going to the store to buy their groceries, it's quite far.
